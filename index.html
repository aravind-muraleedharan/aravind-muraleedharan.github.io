Hi, I’m Aravind

I'm an AI engineer with nearly 7 years of experience taking AI from prototype to production. I build enterprise GenAI systems (LLM applications, retrieval-augmented generation and multi-agent workflows) with a focus on reliability, evaluation and security.

Before GenAI, I worked on large-scale machine learning: translation models for Indian languages trained from scratch, graph neural networks on networks with over 90 million edges, and multilingual pipelines processing a million posts a day, often in secure, air-gapped environments.

I started as a mechanical engineer, earned an M.Tech in Engineering Design and published research in computational fluid dynamics and combustion. That foundation shapes how I work: first principles, careful measurement, and systems that hold up in practice. I'm certified in AI governance and information security (ISO/IEC 42001 and 27001 Lead Auditor), because I care about AI that is not only capable but trustworthy.
